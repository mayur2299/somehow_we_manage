# CREATE 2026 — Official Rules (from create.radix360.site)

## Participation
1. Teams of 2–5 people. No solo entries.
2. Maximum 2 developers or technical builders per team.
3. Everyone can participate. Technical skill is not a prerequisite.
4. Use any AI tools you like. One Claude premium seat per team on request.
5. Teams are single-hub. You'll be building together in one location.

## The day
6. Build starts at 14:00 IST / 12:30 GST on 11 September.
7. Submissions close at 23:59 IST / 22:29 GST. Hard stop.
8. The theme is revealed at the start of the hackathon.
9. Public APIs may be used if declared; the idea must be new.
10. Show us what you built. The best builds walk away with prizes.

## Key dates
- 01 Sept — Registration opens
- 08 Sept — Team creation closes
- 11 Sept — Event start: theme reveal + 10-hour build
- 18 Sept — Showcase

## What makes a great hack? (judging weights)
- 30 — Does it actually work?
- 25 — Is the problem real?
- 25 — Craft and clarity
- 20 — Nerve — did you attempt something hard?
